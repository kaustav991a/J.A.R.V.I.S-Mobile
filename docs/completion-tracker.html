Fixed where jarvisProvider.test.tsx said it had to be: at the source, not inside each .then. state/live.ts is a per-run cancellation scope — a factory rather than a hook, because a provider-lifetime guard cannot cancel a dependency change and the alert registration at [alert?.id, alert] needs exactly that. 20

Promote what he is told into what he knows

Today he learns four things, all about the phone: screen time, pickups, top apps, plus named places. He learns nothing from what you say — the only route from a sentence to a durable fact is typing it into the Memory screen yourself. Meanwhile the chat window holds about a day and rolls silently. The pattern to copy already exists and is proved: shareFacts derives, keeps a ledger of what it already sent, supersedes what changed, and — critically — sends derived facts, never raw rows. A turn-to-fact pass wants the same shape and the same restraint, plus a rule about consent, because quietly distilling everything a person says is a different product from one that remembers what it was told.
